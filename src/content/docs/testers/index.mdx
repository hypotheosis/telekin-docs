Thanks for testing Telekin. For two weeks, your AI tools share one memory about you, your work and your projects. The question this test answers is simple: does your AI start to feel like it already knows your world?

Setup takes about 5 minutes, and your first session about 10. Your invite is an email with a link to set up your account: start with [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/), then follow [Your first 15 minutes](/start/first-15-minutes/) to get going, then use your AI as you normally would.

## What we store and who can see it

- Everything you or your AI write to your memory is stored on Telekin's servers. It's encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest.
- Telekin staff can't open your catalog unless they deliberately unlock it and give a reason, and every unlock shows in your audit log. We normally ask your permission first. Nobody else has access to your catalog.
- Please keep anything your employer considers confidential out of it. See [Keep private material out of Telekin](/how-to/guides/keep-private-material-out/) for what's fine to store.

## When the test ends

You get a full export of your memory as plain Markdown files, and then your data is deleted. If you want to keep using Telekin, say so and it stays.
