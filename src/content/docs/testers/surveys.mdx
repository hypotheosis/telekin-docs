You'll get two short surveys, each taking about 3 to 5 minutes. Results are recorded under an alias, never your name.

## Day 3

Sent three days in. It asks about setup:

- which AI tools you connected, and whether any step failed or confused you;
- whether you normally use ChatGPT, and whether you connected it;
- whether your first session, the interview or a document import, worked and roughly how much it recorded;
- whether an AI has used something from your memory without being asked, and whether one clearly missed something it should have known.

## Day 14

Sent at the end. It asks how the two weeks went:

- how far you agree that your AI tools now know your world better;
- how often an AI used your memory without being asked;
- your best moment and your most frustrating one;
- whether your memory grew on its own after day 1;
- if you used more than one AI tool, whether knowledge carried over between them;
- whether you would pay for Telekin as a hosted service, and what would change your answer;
- whether you would put work context in it, and what you would need to see first;
- anything you would change, and whether we may quote your answers anonymously.

## Tell us how you set up

Say in the survey if you used the [enhanced Claude Code setup](/how-to/connect/claude-code-enhanced/), or added the extra line to your AI's custom instructions. It changes how we read your answers.
