The most useful thing you can send is a moment, good or bad. Moments matter more than any score.

## Moments worth sending

- **Good:** "It remembered X without me asking."
- **Missed:** "It should have known Y and didn't."
- **Growth:** your AI added something to your memory on its own.

Don't mention Telekin when you ask your usual questions; that's what makes these moments tell us something.

## How to send them

Jot each one down as it happens. A note on your phone is fine. Send the notes to the founder whenever suits you. You can also send any one of them through [Send feedback](/feedback/).

## If something breaks

Send the founder a message with a screenshot. Leave your key out of the screenshot. For common fixes, try [Troubleshoot](/how-to/guides/troubleshoot/) first.
