## Is my data used to train AI?

No. Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models. Each AI app you connect has its own policy, though, so check its privacy settings separately. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## Who can see my data?

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest on Telekin's servers. If you contact support, however, with your permission they can read your data to help you figure out a problem. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## What will it cost?

It's free during the private test. Pricing for after the test isn't final. We'll tell you before anything changes.

## Which AIs work with it?

Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and Cline work today. See [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/).

## Can I self-host it?

Yes. Telekin runs on [okf-mcp](https://github.com/hypotheosis/okf_mcp), which is open source under AGPL-3.0. See instructions in that repo for self-hosting.

## Can I delete everything?

Yes! You can <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> to have catalogs deleted. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/) for the details, and [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/).

## Does it work on my phone?

If you set up Telekin in your Claude desktop or web app first, it should show up in Claude's mobile apps. Other apps on phones aren't confirmed yet. See [Connect Claude](/how-to/connect/claude/).

## Does it work offline?

No. Telekin is a hosted server, so your AI needs an internet connection to reach your memory.

## Can my team share a catalog?

Yes. A [catalog](/reference/glossary/#catalog) can belong to a team, and everyone on the team connects their own AIs to it with their own Telekin account, so each person's changes are recorded under their own name. During the private test <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> to set up teams for you.

## How is it different from my AI's built-in memory?

Built-in memory belongs to one app, and you can't really inspect or version it. Telekin is one memory that several AIs share, stored as plain Markdown you can read, undo, edit, and export. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## How is it different from Obsidian or other note tools?

A vault lives on one machine and mixes your notes with your AI's. Telekin is hosted, and keeps the AI's memory separate, with drafts, history and undo. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## How do I get access?

Telekin is in a private test, so access is invite-only right now. 

Once you've received your invite email, see [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/) to get started. Then Claude on the web, ChatGPT and Cursor's one-click install sign in with that account. For other tools you [make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/) yourself. [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) shows what each app needs.
