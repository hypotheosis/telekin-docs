## Is my data used to train AI?

No. Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models. Each AI app you connect has its own policy, though, so check its privacy settings separately. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## Who can see my data?

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest on Telekin's servers. Telekin staff can't open your memory unless they deliberately unlock it and give a reason, and every unlock shows in your audit log. We normally ask your permission first, when you've asked for help with a problem. The exceptions are investigating abuse or answering a lawful request. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## What will it cost?

A personal plan is $8 a month or $85 a year (before tax), after a 30-day free trial. See [pricing](https://telekin.ai/pricing/) for current plans.

## Which AIs work with it?

Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and Cline work today, and Zed should. Almost all of them sign in in your browser with your Telekin account, with no key to copy. See [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/).

## Can I self-host it?

Yes. Telekin runs on [okf-mcp](https://github.com/hypotheosis/okf_mcp), which is open source under AGPL-3.0. See instructions in that repo for self-hosting.

## Can I get a copy of everything?

Yes. In your Telekin console, **Import & export** downloads your whole memory as a `.tar.gz` of Markdown files. See [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/#export-everything).

## Can I delete everything?

Yes! <a href={CONTACT_URL}>Ask us</a> to delete your memory, then delete your account yourself from the **Account** page. See [Delete your account](/how-to/guides/delete-your-account/) and [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## Does it work on my phone?

Two ways. To read your memory yourself, install the Telekin Reader from `https://app.telekin.ai/read/`; it works offline too. See [Read your memory on your phone](/how-to/guides/read-on-your-phone/). For your AI on your phone: if you set up Telekin in your Claude desktop or web app first, it should show up in Claude's mobile apps. Other AI apps on phones aren't confirmed yet. See [Connect Claude](/how-to/connect/claude/).

## Does it work offline?

Your AI needs an internet connection to reach your memory, because Telekin is a hosted server. The [Telekin Reader](/how-to/guides/read-on-your-phone/) keeps copies of the pages you've opened, so you can read those offline.

## Can my team share a catalog?

Yes. A [catalog](/reference/glossary/#catalog) can belong to a team, and everyone on the team connects their own AIs to it with their own Telekin account, so each person's changes are recorded under their own name. For now, <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> to set up teams for you. Once a team is set up, its owners can choose in the console whether members may read or write the team's memories, and give other team members access, without asking us.

## How is it different from my AI's built-in memory?

Built-in memory belongs to one app, and you can't really inspect or version it. Telekin is one memory that several AIs share, stored as plain Markdown you can read, undo, edit, and export. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## How is it different from Obsidian or other note tools?

A vault lives on one machine and mixes your notes with your AI's. Telekin is hosted, and keeps the AI's memory separate, with drafts, history and undo. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## How do I get access?

[Sign up](https://join.telekin.ai/signup) and start your 30-day trial. You'll get an email with a link to set up your account; see [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/). Then your AI apps sign in with that account, in your browser. For the few that can't, you [make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/) yourself. [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) shows what each app needs.
