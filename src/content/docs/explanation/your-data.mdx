Your Telekin memory can hold personal things. Here's how we keep it safe and private.

## Storage

Memories are encrypted in transit using TLS, and encrypted at rest on Telekin's servers.

## Who can see it

You, through your Telekin account, the apps you approved, and the keys you made. If a memory belongs to a team, the team's members can see it too. Telekin support can read catalogs with your permission to debug issues. No other parties have access to your data.

You can see every connection, and revoke any of them, on **My keys** and **My grants** in your Telekin console.

## Training

Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models.

## Your AI client

Telekin is only half the picture. When your AI app reads from Telekin, what you see goes into that chat, and what the app does with it afterwards is governed by that app's own policy. Check the privacy settings of the apps you connect.

## Export and deletion

Both are by request today. <a href={CONTACT_URL}>Ask us</a> and you get a full copy of your catalog, or to have a catalog deleted. A self-serve export is planned.

## Format

Your memory is plain Markdown files, in an open format. You own it and can take it anywhere: any text editor can read it, and nothing about it is locked to Telekin.

Not sure what to keep out? See [Keep private material out of Telekin](/how-to/guides/keep-private-material-out/). Other questions are in the [FAQ](/faq/).
