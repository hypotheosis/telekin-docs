Your Telekin memory can hold personal things. Here's how we keep it safe and private.

## Storage

Memories are encrypted in transit using TLS, and encrypted at rest on Telekin's servers.

## Who can see it

You, through your Telekin account, the apps you approved, and the keys you made. If a memory belongs to a team, the team's members can see it too. Telekin staff can't open your memory unless they deliberately unlock it and give a reason, and every unlock shows in your audit log. We normally ask your permission first, when you've asked for help with a problem. The exceptions are investigating abuse or answering a lawful request. No other parties have access to your data.

You can see every connection, and revoke any of them, on **My keys** and **My grants** in your Telekin console.

## Training

Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models.

## Your AI client

Telekin is only half the picture. When your AI app reads from Telekin, what you see goes into that chat, and what the app does with it afterwards is governed by that app's own policy. Check the privacy settings of the apps you connect.

## Export and deletion

- **Export** is self-serve: in your Telekin console, open **Import & export**, pick your memory and choose **Export .tar.gz**. You get every folder and Markdown file. See [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/#export-everything).
- **Deleting a memory** is by request today: <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a>.
- **Deleting your account** is self-serve, from the **Account** page, once your own memory has been deleted. See [Delete your account](/how-to/guides/delete-your-account/).
- **Connections** that go unused for 30 days are disconnected automatically, so a forgotten app doesn't keep access.

## Format

Your memory is plain Markdown files, in an open format. You own it and can take it anywhere: any text editor can read it, and nothing about it is locked to Telekin.

Not sure what to keep out? See [Keep private material out of Telekin](/how-to/guides/keep-private-material-out/). Other questions are in the [FAQ](/faq/).
