You connected Telekin, you asked a question it should have known, and your AI answered as if it had never heard of you. The memory is there. The AI didn't look. This page explains why, and what helps.

## Why AIs don't always check

Connecting Telekin gives your AI a set of tools. An AI only uses a tool when something prompts it to. Telekin's tool descriptions tell the AI to look when you ask what it remembers or refer to something you told it before, but not every app follows tool descriptions closely, and a question that doesn't sound like either gives it no reason to look. Unless your question or your settings point that way, the AI may answer from what it already knows and never open the tool.

Long chats make it worse, since instructions given early can fade as the conversation grows. This is especially common with longer context windows.

## What helps

- **Custom instructions.** Add one short instruction to your AI app's settings, telling it to search Telekin whenever you mention a project, system or person, and to record what is worth keeping. See [Get your AI to use Telekin without being asked](/how-to/guides/use-without-asking/).
- **Name things in your question.** A question that names a project or system gives your AI something to search for; "why is it slow?" doesn't. Examples are in the [guide's habits](/how-to/guides/use-without-asking/#habits-that-help).
- **Ask it to check.** Telling it to check Telekin before answering works every time — the [Prompt library](/reference/prompt-library/#check-before-answering) has a ready prompt.
- **Hooks.** Claude Code can run a small startup script that reminds it, at the start of every session, which catalog is yours. Together with instructions in its global `CLAUDE.md` file, it makes Claude Code check Telekin on its own. See [Connect Claude Code, enhanced](/how-to/connect/claude-code-enhanced/).

## What Telekin does on its side

Telekin's tool descriptions now steer the AI on their own: they tell it to search your memory when you ask what it remembers or refer to something you told it before, and to save things worth keeping that you share, without being asked, telling you what it saved. Apps that follow tool descriptions closely do more of this without help. Not every app does, so the steps above stay the reliable way.
