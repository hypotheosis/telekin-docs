New to Telekin? Read [What is Telekin?](/explanation/what-is-telekin/) first: it takes a few minutes and explains the ideas behind it.

To begin, [sign up](https://join.telekin.ai/signup) and start your 30-day trial. Telekin sends you an email with a link to set up your account. Open it first: [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/) shows how, and where to find your Telekin address afterwards.

Then try the tutorials. Each one walks you through a small, complete project so you learn by doing.
