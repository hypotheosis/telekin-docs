This walks you through the core Telekin workflow. Your AI will have written down what it learned about you and your work, and a brand-new chat will be able to pick up that info all on its own.

## Set up your account

You'll need a Telekin account. [Sign up](https://join.telekin.ai/signup) to start your 30-day trial, then [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/) walks you through the setup email.

<AgentPrompt prompt={firstSetup}>
Short on time? Once you have your account, start a new chat with your AI and give it this prompt. It walks you through connecting Telekin, checks the connection, and runs the interview, one step at a time.
</AgentPrompt>

This page uses Claude on the web (claude.ai) because it doesn't need a terminal. If you use something else, pick it from [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) and come back for the later steps.

## Connect Claude

<Steps>

1. Go to **Customize, then Connectors** (claude.ai/customize/connectors).
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Telekin`, paste `https://app.telekin.ai/mcp` as the **Server URL**, and click **Continue**. If you're asked how to authenticate, choose **OAuth**. Leave any client ID or client secret fields empty.
4. Claude sends you to Telekin to approve its access. Sign in if you're asked. The page, **Allow access?**, says what **Approve** gives Claude: with one memory (Telekin's screens call it a catalog), that's **write** access to it, as the default. That's what you want, so choose **Approve**. Your browser returns to Claude, now connected. (**Deny** connects nothing.)
5. In a chat, click **+**, then **Connectors**, and make sure `Telekin` is on.

</Steps>

You should now see `Telekin` in the connectors list, switched on for that chat.

If you have more than one memory, the choices on **Allow access?** start open, under **Advanced Options**: tick the memory, choose **write**, pick the same memory as the **Default catalog**, then **Approve**.

## Add one line to your preferences

Claude only uses Telekin when it thinks of it, so give it a nudge. Add the one line from [Get your AI to use Telekin without being asked](/how-to/guides/use-without-asking/) to your custom instructions — that page also shows where to find them in Claude.

## Let it interview you

Start a new chat and paste the interview prompt from the [Prompt library](/reference/prompt-library/#the-interview).

Answer as much or as little as you like. Claude asks one question at a time and, every few answers, saves what it learned. Claude may ask your permission before each save; allow it. At the end, ask "What did you record?" to see what went in.

A [changeset](/reference/glossary/#changeset-draft) is just a batch of edits Claude drafts in one go.

## See it remember

<Steps>

1. Start a **new chat**, so nothing from the interview is in front of Claude.
2. Ask: **"What do you know about me?"**

</Steps>

You should see Claude search Telekin and then summarize what it found, in its own words. Now ask a work question that names one of your projects. Notice whether it uses what it already knows instead of asking you to explain.
