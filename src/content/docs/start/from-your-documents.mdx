You probably already have context written down locally: a README, AGENTS.md, your notes, a project brief, your own bio, etc. 

This tutorial shows you how to read local documents into Telekin.

<Aside type="note" title="Set up Telekin first!">
You'll need an AI app already connected to Telekin. If you haven't done that yet, start with [Your first 15 minutes](/start/first-15-minutes/).
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep it non-confidential">
  Don't paste anything your employer considers confidential. See [Keep private material out of Telekin](/how-to/guides/keep-private-material-out/) for what's fine to store.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Pick one document** you have on hand, such as a README, a project brief or your own bio. It's okay if it's short.

2. **Start a new chat and paste the document.** Under it, say the "fill it from a document" line from the [Prompt library](/reference/prompt-library/#fill-it-from-a-document).

   (Here "[catalog](/reference/glossary/#catalog)" means your Telekin memory.)

   Your AI works out which facts will still matter later, and saves them to Telekin. Allow it to save if it asks permission.

3. **Ask what it saved.** Type "What did you record?" You should get a list of what went in. If something is missing or wrong, say so and ask the AI to fix it.

4. **Open a new chat and ask about it.** Choose something the document covered and ask about it without pasting anything. You should see your AI search Telekin and answer from what it recorded.

</Steps>

That's the whole loop: paste, record, ask. Repeat with another document whenever you like.
