You can do most of this by asking your AI, which uses Telekin's tools. You can also look for yourself, in your Telekin console or in the [Telekin Reader](/how-to/guides/read-on-your-phone/) on your phone.

## Review what was saved

Ask:

- **"What did you record in Telekin today?"**
- **"Show me the history of `<concept>`."** Replace `<concept>` with the name of the item you care about, such as a project.
- **"What has `<person>` changed in Telekin this week?"** Useful in a team memory, to see one person's changes.
- **"What changed in Telekin since Monday?"** Lists everything published since that day, whoever made it.

Telekin keeps a history of every change, with a message, when, and the person whose account made it.

To look yourself, sign in to your Telekin console at `https://app.telekin.ai`:

- **Recent changes** lists everything published in your memory, newest first. Choose a row to see exactly what changed.
- **Browse** shows your memory's folders and pages.
- The search box at the top of the sidebar finds pages by their words.

The Reader's **Activity** timeline shows the same changes on your phone.

## Undo a change

Ask: **"Revert your last change to `<concept>`."**

Your AI looks the change up in the history and reverts it. The old version comes back, as a new change you can see in the history.

## Export everything

You can download a full copy of your memory yourself:

1. In your Telekin console, open **Import & export**.
2. Pick your memory and choose **Export .tar.gz**.

You get a `.tar.gz` archive of your memory's folders and Markdown files, which any computer can open. What else to know about your data is in [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).
