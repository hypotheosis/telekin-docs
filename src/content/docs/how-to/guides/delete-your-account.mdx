You can delete your Telekin account yourself, from the **Account** page in your Telekin console. Deleting it removes your sign-in (password, passkeys), your sessions, every key you made and every app you connected.

<Aside type="caution" title="Your memory comes first">
Telekin won't delete an account while that account still owns a memory, so that a memory is never left without an owner. Your own memory counts. So, before you delete your account, export your memory if you want a copy, then <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> to delete it.
</Aside>

## Delete it

<Steps>

1. **Keep a copy, if you want one.** In the console, open **Import & export**, pick your memory and choose **Export .tar.gz**. See [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/#export-everything).
2. **Have your memory deleted.** <a href={CONTACT_URL}>Ask us</a> to delete it. If a memory belongs to a team you share, it stays with the team.
3. **Delete the account.** In the console, open **Account** and scroll to **Delete your account**. Confirm it's you in one of three ways:
   - type your current password and choose **Delete my account**;
   - choose **Confirm with a passkey and delete my account**;
   - if you sign in through another provider, choose **Re-authenticate with** *provider* **to delete my account**. The provider asks you to sign in again, then sends you back to the **Account** page, which checks with Telekin that the provider confirmed a fresh sign-in. If it did, the page shows **Delete my account**; choose it within a few minutes. If it didn't, the page says so straight away and offers **Try again** instead (see [below](#if-its-refused)).

   Type your account ID where the page asks, then confirm.

</Steps>

You're signed out, and the sign-in page says **Your account was deleted.** This can't be undone.

## If it's refused

The page says why, and nothing is deleted. Most of these come up when you choose to delete; a provider that didn't confirm a fresh sign-in is reported as soon as you're back on the **Account** page:

- **Your account still owns a memory.** Do step 2 first.
- **You're the only owner of a shared team.** Make someone else an owner of the team first, or ask us.
- **The provider didn't confirm a fresh sign-in.** Re-authenticating counts only if you really signed in at the provider just now; some providers skip that when you're already signed in there. The page shows this when the provider sends you back, before offering **Delete my account**. Sign out of the provider, sign back in, then choose **Try again: re-authenticate with** *provider*.
- **The page has no way to confirm it's you**: no password, no passkey and no linked sign-in account it can use. The page says to set a password, add a passkey or link a sign-in account first; do one of those on the **Account** page, then come back.

The history of a team's memory keeps the changes you made, under your name as it was written; your account itself is gone.
