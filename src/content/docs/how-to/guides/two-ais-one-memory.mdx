Your memory lives in Telekin, not in any one app. Connect a second app and both see the same memory.

## Connect a second AI

Pick another app from [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) and follow its guide. Use the **same address** (your Telekin address with `/mcp` at the end) you used for the first one. When it signs in in your browser, approve the **same memory** you chose for the first app (with only one memory, that's what **Approve** gives it anyway). Each app gets its own connection on **My grants**, so you can disconnect one without the other. If an app needs a key instead, [make one](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/) for it that reaches the same memory.

## Try it

<Steps>

1. In your first AI (say Claude), tell it something worth remembering: **"Remember that I prefer short, plain replies and that I'm working on the Orchard project this month."**
2. Ask it to save the fact if it hasn't, and wait for it to say it has.
3. Open your second AI (say Cursor) and ask: **"What do you know about me?"**

</Steps>

The second AI should find the same facts and use them, even though you never told it.

## Good to know

Both AIs write to the same memory, so either one can add to or change what the other saved. Every change is kept in the history with a message and a time, so you can see what changed and undo it. See [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/).
