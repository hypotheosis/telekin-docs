The **Telekin Reader** is a small app for reading your memory yourself, on your phone or any browser. It lives at `https://app.telekin.ai/read/` and signs in with your Telekin account. It only reads: nothing you do in it changes your memory.

## Install it

<Steps>

1. On your phone, open `https://app.telekin.ai/read/`.
2. Install it:
   - **iPhone and iPad:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android:** in Chrome, accept the install prompt, or install it from Chrome's menu.
3. Open **Telekin** from your home screen and choose **Sign in**. Sign in with your Telekin account; a passkey makes this quick. You're sent back to the Reader.

</Steps>

On iPhone and iPad the installed app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari's, so sign in once inside it.

If your account reaches one memory, the Reader opens it straight away. With more than one, pick it from the list; tap the memory's name at the top to switch later.

## What you can do

The Reader has four tabs at the bottom:

- **Home:** a search box, **Jump back in** (the last pages you opened on this device), and **Activity**, a timeline of what changed, who changed it and when. Filter it to **Created**, **Edited**, **Deleted** or **By me**.
- **Browse:** your memory's folders and pages. A folder shows what it's about, its subfolders and its pages, and a **Log** of changes.
- **Search:** finds pages that match your words, with a short snippet of each.
- **Account:** who you're signed in as, **Appearance** (System, Light or Dark), **Sign out** and **Delete offline copies**.

A page shows its type and tags, how long it takes to read, who edited it last, an **On this page** outline, the whole text, and **Linked from**: the other pages that link to it.

## Offline

The Reader keeps a copy of each page you open, up to 200, so you can read them without a connection. Offline, Home lists them under **Available offline**, with when each was last synced. Search needs a connection.

<Aside type="note" title="Leaving a shared phone">
**Sign out** on the Account tab ends your session. **Delete offline copies** removes the pages kept on this device. If Telekin signs you out, for example because your account's access changed, the Reader deletes its offline copies by itself.
</Aside>

## Make changes through your AI

To add to or change your memory, ask your AI, as usual. The Reader shows the result as soon as it's published. To see the same pages on a computer, the console at `https://app.telekin.ai` has **Browse** and **Recent changes** too, and a search box at the top of the sidebar.
