You'll need: your Telekin address with `/mcp` at the end (the `<server URL>` below, `https://app.telekin.ai/mcp`), your Telekin account ([Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/)), and, if your app doesn't sign in with OAuth, a key you make for it ([Make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/)).

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the standard way AI apps talk to tools like Telekin. If your app supports remote MCP servers, it can probably connect. Here is everything it needs.

## What to set

Telekin accepts two ways to sign in, side by side, at the same URL. Most apps that support remote MCP servers now support OAuth, so try that first.

**If your app supports remote MCP servers with OAuth, sign in in the browser.**

<Steps>

1. **URL:** your `<server URL>`, which ends in `/mcp`.
2. **Authentication:** choose OAuth, and leave any client ID and client secret fields empty.
3. The app opens Telekin's page in a browser. Sign in with your Telekin account if you're asked. On **Allow access?**, the page says what **Approve** gives the app: with one memory (Telekin's screens call memories catalogs), **write** access to it, as the default. With more than one, choose under **Advanced Options**: tick each memory, choose **read** or **write** for each and pick a **Default catalog**. Then choose **Approve**. The connection then shows on **My grants** in your Telekin console, where you can revoke it.

</Steps>

**Otherwise, use a key you make, in a header.**

<Steps>

1. **Transport:** "Streamable HTTP" (sometimes just "HTTP").
2. **URL:** your `<server URL>`, which ends in `/mcp`.
3. **Header:** the name `Authorization`, with the value `Bearer <your key>`. Include the word `Bearer` and a space before the key.

</Steps>

You don't need a catalog header.

**Where to look in your app:** search its docs for "remote MCP server", "MCP headers" or "OAuth".

If your app only supports local (stdio) servers, it can't connect.

## Check it works

Ask your AI: **"What tools do you have from Telekin?"** You should see a list of tools for searching and writing your memory. If it can't connect, check that the URL ends in `/mcp`, and, if you use a key, that the header value starts with `Bearer `.

## Undo it

Remove the server from your app's MCP settings. Then, in your Telekin console, revoke its key on **My keys**, or its connection on **My grants** if it signed in in the browser.
