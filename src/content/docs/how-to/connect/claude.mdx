You'll need: your Telekin address with `/mcp` at the end, and your Telekin account ([Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/)).

<Waitlist />

Claude signs in in your browser with your Telekin account, so there's nothing to copy or paste. You choose which memories it may use, and you can disconnect it at any time from **My grants** in your Telekin console.

## Add the connector

<Steps>

1. Go to **Customize, then Connectors** (claude.ai/customize/connectors).
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Telekin`, paste your Telekin address with `/mcp` on the end as the **Server URL**, and click **Continue**. If you're asked how to authenticate, choose **OAuth** (a way of signing in through a page in your browser; there's no password to paste). Leave any client ID or client secret fields empty.
4. Claude opens Telekin's page in your browser. If you're not signed in, sign in with your Telekin account first.
5. The page, **Allow access?**, names the app that's connecting and says what **Approve** gives it. With one memory (Telekin's screens call memories catalogs), that's **write** access to it, as the default catalog, so Claude can save what it learns. Choose **Approve**. With more than one memory, the choices start open under **Advanced Options**: tick each memory Claude may use, choose **read** or **write** for each, pick a **Default catalog** (the memory it uses when it doesn't name one), then **Approve**. Your browser returns to Claude, now connected. (**Deny** connects nothing.)
6. In a chat, click **+**, then **Connectors**, and make sure `Telekin` is on.

</Steps>

Claude Desktop uses the same connectors and the same settings page. If the sign-in page expires before you finish, see [Troubleshoot](/how-to/guides/troubleshoot/).

A few things worth knowing:

- Free plans allow one custom connector.
- Once connected, Claude stays connected on its own as long as you use it at least once every 30 days. After 30 days unused, Telekin disconnects it, and you connect it again the same way.
- On Team and Enterprise plans, a connector an owner adds to the organization connects everyone through one sign-in, so everyone would see and change the same memory. Don't add Telekin that way. Add it as your own connector instead: each person signs in with their own Telekin account. To share a memory with your team, use a Telekin team, which keeps each person's changes under their own name (see the [FAQ](/faq/)).
- Claude connects from Anthropic's servers, so the server URL must be reachable from the public internet.

**On your phone:** connectors you add on the web or desktop should appear in the Claude mobile apps after you next sign in. Set it up on the web or desktop first.

## Check it works

In a chat with `Telekin` switched on, ask: **"What tools do you have from Telekin?"** You should see Claude list tools for searching and saving to your memory. Then ask **"What do you know about me?"**; on an empty memory it will tell you there's nothing yet.

If the connector won't connect, check that the URL ends in `/mcp`. If signing in or approving doesn't work, see [Troubleshoot](/how-to/guides/troubleshoot/). If Claude says it has no tools, make sure the connector is toggled on in this chat (**+**, then **Connectors**).

## Undo it

Go to **Customize, then Connectors**, open `Telekin` and remove it. Nothing is deleted from your memory; it just stops being connected to Claude. You can also disconnect it from Telekin's side: in your Telekin console, open **My grants**, choose the Claude connection, then **Revoke**. To connect again later, add the connector again and approve it.
