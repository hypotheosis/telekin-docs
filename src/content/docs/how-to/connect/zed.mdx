You'll need: your Telekin account ([Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/)).

<Waitlist />

Zed signs in to remote MCP servers with OAuth, so it signs in in your browser with your Telekin account and there's no key to copy or paste.

## Add the server

<Steps>

1. In Zed, open **Settings**, then **AI**, then **MCP Servers**.
2. Choose **Add Server**, then **Add Remote Server**.
3. Name it `telekin` and enter `https://app.telekin.ai/mcp` as the URL. Zed writes an entry with just that `url` to its settings. Don't add an `Authorization` header.
4. Zed asks you to authenticate and opens Telekin's page in your browser. If you're not signed in to Telekin, sign in. The page, **Allow access?**, says what **Approve** gives Zed: with one memory (Telekin's screens call memories catalogs), that's **write** access to it, as the default. Choose **Approve**. With more than one memory, choose under **Advanced Options** first. (**Deny** connects nothing.)

</Steps>

Zed asks you to confirm tool actions by default, so expect it to ask before Telekin saves anything.

## Check it works

In Zed's agent panel, ask: **"What tools do you have from Telekin?"** You should see a list. Then ask **"What do you know about me?"**; on an empty memory it will say there's nothing yet.

If it won't connect, check that the URL ends in `/mcp`. If you don't see a sign-in prompt, <a href={CONTACT_URL}>tell us</a> what Zed showed.

## Undo it

Remove `telekin` from Zed's MCP servers. To disconnect it from Telekin's side, open **My grants** in your Telekin console, choose the Zed connection, then **Revoke**.
