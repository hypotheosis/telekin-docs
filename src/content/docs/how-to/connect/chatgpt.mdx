You'll need: your Telekin address with `/mcp` at the end, and your Telekin account ([Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/)).

<Waitlist />

ChatGPT signs in in your browser with your Telekin account, so there's nothing to copy or paste. You choose which memories it may use, and you can disconnect it at any time from **My grants** in your Telekin console.

## Add the server

Custom MCP servers are added in ChatGPT on the web. Menu names change, so look for them rather than expecting these exact words.

<Steps>

1. In ChatGPT on the web, look for **Plugins** (or a similar place for custom MCP servers).
2. Create a new one. Give it a name such as `Telekin`, an optional description, and paste your Telekin address with `/mcp` on the end as the **Server URL**.
3. For authentication, choose **OAuth** (a way of signing in through a page in your browser; there's no password to paste). Leave any client ID or client secret fields empty.
4. Create it, then install it.
5. ChatGPT opens Telekin's page in your browser. If you're not signed in, sign in with your Telekin account first.
6. The page, **Allow access?**, names the app that's connecting and says what **Approve** gives it. With one memory (Telekin's screens call memories catalogs), that's **write** access to it, as the default catalog, so ChatGPT can save what it learns. Choose **Approve**. With more than one memory, the choices start open under **Advanced Options**: tick each memory ChatGPT may use, choose **read** or **write** for each, pick a **Default catalog** (the memory it uses when it doesn't name one), then **Approve**. Your browser returns to ChatGPT, now connected. (**Deny** connects nothing.)

</Steps>

Once connected, ChatGPT stays connected on its own as long as you use it at least once every 30 days. After 30 days unused, Telekin disconnects it, and you connect it again the same way. If the sign-in page expires before you finish, see [Troubleshoot](/how-to/guides/troubleshoot/).

Two things may get in your way. Which ChatGPT plans include custom MCP servers isn't confirmed, and if you're in a workspace, an admin can restrict adding them.

## Check it works

In a new chat, ask: **"What tools do you have from Telekin?"** You should see ChatGPT list tools for searching and saving to your memory. Then ask **"What do you know about me?"**; on an empty memory it will tell you there's nothing yet.

ChatGPT asks for your confirmation before each change it makes to your memory. That is expected. Approve the ones you want.

If it won't connect, check that the URL ends in `/mcp`. If signing in or approving doesn't work, see [Troubleshoot](/how-to/guides/troubleshoot/).

## Undo it

Remove the Telekin server from the same place in ChatGPT where you added it. Nothing is deleted from your memory; it just stops being connected to ChatGPT. You can also disconnect it from Telekin's side: in your Telekin console, open **My grants**, choose the ChatGPT connection, then **Revoke**. To connect again later, install it again and approve it.
