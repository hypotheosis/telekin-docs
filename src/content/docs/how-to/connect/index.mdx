One short guide per AI app, each showing how to connect that app to your memory. Pick the app you use and follow the numbered steps.

Every app connects to the same address: `https://app.telekin.ai/mcp`. You'll need your Telekin account first; see [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/).

Almost every app now **signs in in the browser**. The app opens Telekin's page, you sign in with your Telekin account and choose **Approve**, and you're sent back, connected. There's no key to copy or paste. You can see and disconnect every app on **My grants** in your Telekin console.

**A key you make** is still there for the few cases that need one: an app that can't sign in with OAuth, a machine with no browser (a server you reach over SSH, a container), or a script. See [Make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/).

To read your memory yourself on your phone, you don't need an AI app at all: see [Read your memory on your phone](/how-to/guides/read-on-your-phone/).
