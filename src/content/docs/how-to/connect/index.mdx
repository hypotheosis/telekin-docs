One short guide per AI app, each showing how to connect that app to your memory. Pick the app you use and follow the numbered steps.

Every app connects to the same address: `https://app.telekin.ai/mcp`. You'll need your Telekin account first; see [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/).

Apps connect in one of two ways:

- **Sign in in the browser.** Claude on the web and desktop, and ChatGPT, open Telekin's page in your browser. You sign in with your Telekin account, choose which memories the app may use, and approve. There's nothing to copy or paste.
- **A key you make.** Developer tools (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code and the like) send a key with every request. You make one for each tool on your **My keys** page; see [Make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/).
