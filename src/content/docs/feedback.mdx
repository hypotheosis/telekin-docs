Something broken, confusing or missing? Something you like? Tell us here. Every message goes to the people building Telekin.

<FeedbackForm />

You can also choose **Send feedback** in your Telekin console, or ask your AI to send it: say "Send feedback to Telekin that..." and it uses Telekin's `send_feedback` tool.

When a Telekin tool fails with an internal error that isn't a passing outage (such as a brief database hiccup), the error tells your AI it can report the problem with `send_feedback`, quoting the error's trace id when there is one, so we can find the failing request. So your AI may offer to send a report, or mention that it can, without being asked. If you'd rather it didn't, just tell it not to.
