## Catalog

Your whole collection of notes in Telekin. It is a set of folders and Markdown files.

## Concept

One page in a catalog, about one project, system, person or decision. Concepts link to each other. See [What a concept looks like](/reference/what-a-concept-looks-like/).

## Changeset (draft)

A set of changes your AI has prepared but not yet made real. Nothing in a changeset is visible to anyone, including your other AIs, until it is published. You can look at it or throw it away.

## Publish

Save a changeset to the catalog. Every publish is saved with a short message, so it can be found and undone later.

## Provenance

Where a fact came from: who wrote it, when, and from what source (a document, a conversation, a check against the real thing).

## MCP

The [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2026-07-28/getting-started/intro), an open standard that lets an AI app use outside tools. Telekin works through it. If an app supports MCP servers, it can connect to Telekin.

## Connector

What some apps (Claude, for example) call a connection to an MCP server. Adding Telekin to such an app means adding a custom connector.

## Account

How you sign in to Telekin, with your email and a password, or a passkey. You set it up from the link in your setup email. Your account decides which catalogs you, and the apps you connect, can reach. See [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/).

## Console

Telekin's web pages, at `https://app.telekin.ai`. You sign in there to browse your memory (**Browse**, and **Recent changes**, which opens with a summary of your memory's health) and search it from the box at the top of the sidebar, download it (**Import & export**), see and revoke app connections (**My grants**) and keys (**My keys**), and look after your password, passkeys, sessions and display name (**Account**).

## Grant

An app connection you approved in your browser, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code. It reaches the catalogs you approved, at the level you chose. Grants are listed on **My grants** in the console, with when each was last active, and you can revoke one there. A grant unused for 30 days is revoked automatically.

## Key

A secret that lets an app reach your catalogs without signing in in the browser, for apps or machines that can't. You make one for each app on **My keys** in the console, and it's shown only once. See [Make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/).

## OAuth

A standard way for an app to be signed in without you handing it a password or key. The app sends you to a sign-in page in your browser, you approve it, and you're sent back. In Telekin, you sign in with your account and approve which catalogs the app may use. Almost every app connects this way.

## Display name

The readable name of a catalog, team or person, such as "Alex's notes", shown on Telekin's pages and in what your AI sees, beside the short name (such as `t01`) that settings use. You can change your own on the **Account** page.

## Reader

The Telekin Reader, a read-only app at `https://app.telekin.ai/read/` for reading your memory on your phone, offline too. See [Read your memory on your phone](/how-to/guides/read-on-your-phone/).

## OKF

The Open Knowledge Format: plain Markdown files with a small YAML block of details at the top. It is the format Telekin stores your memory in.

## okf-mcp

The open-source server that Telekin runs on. It speaks MCP to your AI app and stores your catalog as OKF. You will see its name in some setup commands and tool names.
